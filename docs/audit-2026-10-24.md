# App audit — 2026-10-24

Scope: every screen, tested by hand in the browser at desktop (1280 px), phone (375 px) and narrow phone (320 px) sizes, plus the automated suite (57 unit and 18 browser tests, all passing). Each part is checked against the brief (*HSK 3.0 Sentence & Exam Trainer — Build Spec*).

## 1. Bugs found while testing

| # | Where | What happens | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| B1 ✅ fixed | Practice → Writing Part 2 answer box | Typing "xue" leaves only **"e"** in the box, because the box keeps just the last character typed. This will probably also break a Chinese pinyin keyboard (IME) on desktop and Android while you're still choosing the character. | Wait until the IME has finished before trimming (`compositionend`). Accept only Chinese characters and show a hint otherwise. |
| B2 🔴 | Characters → pinyin and meaning label | It shows the *first* dictionary reading, which is often wrong for HSK: **么 → "má"** (should be *me*), **要 → "yāo"** (should be *yào*), **和 → "old variant of 和"**, **为 → "wéi"** (wèi is also common). | Prefer the reading that matches the syllabus pinyin, and skip "variant of" entries. The data build already has the syllabus pinyin. |
| B3 ✅ fixed | Practice, any mode | Leaving the Practice page (any tab) mid-exam **silently loses every answer**. A page reload does the same. | Save the exam in progress to IndexedDB and offer "Resume", or at least warn before leaving. |
| B4 🔴 | Whole app (after deploy) | With `autoUpdate`, a new version can **reload the page by itself**, including in the middle of a timed exam. | Switch to "update available — reload when ready". |
| B5 🔴 | Dictionary entry for unknown text (e.g. `#/entry/abc`) | It shows **Save** for something that isn't in the dictionary, and saves it. | Hide Save when there's no entry. |
| B6 🔴 | Dictionary: traditional characters | 學, 電腦 → "not in the offline dictionary", even though the pack contains their traditional forms. | Index the traditional forms as well. |
| B7 ✅ fixed | Practice → "Finish section" | Finishes with no warning even when 14 of 15 questions are unanswered. | Confirm: "3 unanswered — finish anyway?" |

## 2. Screen-by-screen results

### Home
- ✅ Countdown is right: 50 days to 13 Dec 2026.
- 🟠 Exam date and level are **hard-coded**. There's no Settings screen, so you can't change them (brief §6, *Downloads & Settings*).
- 🟠 There's no "continue where you left off", daily goal, reviews due or recommended practice (brief §6, *Home*).
- 🟢 On desktop the page is mostly empty below the three cards. Room for today's plan and progress.

### Lookup (handwriting)
- ✅ Drawing with mouse, touch or pen, Undo and Clear all work. Recognition runs on-device and works offline.
- ✅ Recognises correctly drawn characters well: 十, 人, 大, 三, 中 and 日 were all first choice. 口 came second, after 囗.
- 🔴/🟢 **Wrong stroke order fails badly.** 十 drawn with the vertical stroke first doesn't offer 十 at all. That's a limit of the recogniser; we can soften it (see I1).
- 🟢 Rare radicals (匸, 丄, 乛, 丩) crowd the 8 suggestions ahead of common characters (I1).
- 🟢 There's no way to look up by **pinyin, English or typed characters**, the obvious fallback when drawing fails.

### Dictionary entry
- ✅ Pinyin with tone marks, definitions, traditional form, HSK level tag, all readings for characters with several, real dictionary words containing the character (HSK-tagged first), stroke-order animation and Save all work.
- 🔴 B5, B6 above.
- 🟢 Pinyin is shown syllable by syllable: "yī huì r" instead of the textbook **yíhuìr**, and "diàn nǎo" instead of **diànnǎo**.
- 🟢 Raw dictionary codes show through: `CL:臺|台[tai2]`, `also pr. [yi1 hui3 r5]`. Format these as "Measure word: 台 tái".
- 🟠 **No example sentences** (brief §3.2, §3.3). A Tatoeba (CC BY 2.0 FR) subset is the planned source.
- 🟠 **No radical, stroke count or component breakdown** (brief §3.2). The Make Me a Hanzi data has all three.
- 🟠 **No pronunciation audio** (brief §3.2). This waits on the listening decision.
- 🟢 The back link always says "Draw another character", even when you came from a word list or the Characters screen.

### Practice (exam)
- ✅ 3 sets × 25 original questions, all labelled. Guided mode (check, then explanation, answer locked). Timed mode (section timers, no feedback until the end, review screen, double-submit blocked). History saved.
- ✅ Format matches the official HSK 2 sample paper for Reading P2–P4 and Writing P1–P2.
- 🔴 B1, B3, B7 above.
- 🟠 **No Listening section** (25 of the 60 real exam questions) and **no Reading Part 1** (picture matching). These are blocked on the audio and picture decision.
- 🟠 **No full mock exam** in the real order and timing (Listening → answer sheet → Reading → Writing, about 60 min).
- 🟠 **No mistake notebook.** Wrong answers are stored but never shown again (brief §2.5).
- 🟠 No error categories (vocabulary, grammar, word order and so on), confidence rating or spaced review (brief §2.5).
- 🟢 In timed mode you can't flag a question to come back to.
- 🟢 The review screen shows everything. Add a "show only mistakes" filter.
- 🟢 75 questions will run out quickly in 7 weeks. We need more sets, and ideally a native speaker's review.

### Characters (writing trainer)
- ✅ All 100 official writing characters. Watch, Trace and From-memory modes. Learned/practising/new status. 10-character sessions. Works offline. Progress survives a reload.
- 🔴 B2 above (wrong reading shown).
- 🟢 A session summary shows results but doesn't schedule the misses for later (spaced review).
- 🟢 There's no "words using this character" practice, such as writing 学 inside 学校 the way Writing Part 2 asks.
- 🟢 Grading leniency hasn't been tested with a finger on a real phone.

### Sources & Licences
- ✅ All six sources are listed with licence and link. The local Arphic licence file opens.
- 🟢 The app's own GPL licence text isn't linked from the screen.

## Update — fixes and features since the audit

- **B1 fixed.** The answer box waits for the pinyin keyboard to finish, keeps only Chinese characters, and shows a hint for Latin letters. Unit-tested with simulated IME events.
- **B3 fixed.** Exams save after every answer (IndexedDB). Practice shows **Resume / Discard**, "Save and leave" is available, and starting another paper asks first. The timed-mode timer **pauses while you're away**: it saves every second and continues where it stopped. (The real exam doesn't pause.)
- **B7 fixed.** Finishing a section with unanswered questions asks "N questions unanswered — finish anyway?". When the timer runs out, the section still ends straight away, as in the real exam.
- **Mistake notebook** (`#/mistakes`). Every question whose latest answer was wrong (blank counts as wrong), with your answer, the right answer, the explanation, how many times you got it wrong, and its category (brief §2.5). You can filter by category. **"Redo mistakes"** runs them as a guided paper; answer correctly and they move to "Fixed".
- **Full mock exams ×3.** Official order and part sizes for Reading P2 (5), P3 (2 × 5) and P4 (5) in 20 min, then Writing P1–P2 (10) in 10 min, with questions numbered through the paper like the real one. Built from sets A–C, rotated so each mock is different. Listening and Reading P1 are still missing.
- Tests: 73 unit and 30 browser tests pass (also under `/hsk-trainer/`).
- Still open from the audit: B2, B4, B5, B6, the accessibility items, and everything in §4 not ticked here.
